Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who is responsible for your information

UltraMe is run by Édouard Verreault, in Québec. I’m the person responsible for the protection of personal information, under Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

What the app collects

Only what your plan needs:

  • Your account: your username and email address.
  • Your profile: your sex (if you choose to give it) and your age group.
  • Your race: distance, date or training length, road or trail, elevation, race name and goal, and a summary of the course if you import its GPX file (distance, elevation, climbs and descents, without the GPS track).
  • Your training: your recent times, your volume, your recent runs, your longest run, your available days and hours and your preferences (units, strength).
  • Your health: injuries in the last 12 months, current pain, and in your check-ins after each session, your fatigue and pain.
  • Your Garmin activities, only if you connect your account (see “Garmin connection” below).
  • Your subscription: its status (trial, active, expired). I never see your card number.

The app doesn’t collect your location, contacts, photos or browsing history, and doesn’t keep the GPS track of your Garmin activities. It contains no ads and no ad tracking tools.

Why I use it

To create your plan, adjust it each week based on your check-ins, manage your account and subscription, and contact you about the service (for example, the reminder before your trial ends).

Your health information is only used to adapt your plan and limit injury risk. I never sell or rent your information, and I don’t use it for advertising.

Your consent

By creating your account, you consent to this use. Your health information is sensitive: you choose to give it, and it’s only used for your plan.

You can withdraw your consent anytime by deleting your account. Without this information, I can no longer create a plan for you.

Who it’s shared with

A few providers run the app. They only access what they need to provide their service:

  • Supabase: hosts your account and data, on servers in North America.
  • Anthropic (Claude), in the United States: creates and adjusts your plan and powers Kova’s replies. I only send it your running, training and health data, and your messages to Kova, never your name or email.
  • Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play): process your payment.
  • RevenueCat, in the United States: manages your subscription status from an anonymous ID.
  • Garmin, only if you connect your account: sends me your activities and receives the sessions and courses you choose to send to your watch.

Before transferring information outside Québec, I assess that it remains adequately protected, as required by Law 25.

Garmin connection (optional)

You can connect your Garmin Connect account. It’s optional: without Garmin, you fill in your check-in by hand.

If you connect it, I only receive activities done after the connection:

  • type, date, duration, distance and pace;
  • elevation gain and loss;
  • your average and maximum heart rate.

This data is only used to fill in your check-in and adjust your plan. I don’t keep the GPS track of your activities.

If you ask, the app can also send your sessions and your race course to your Garmin account, so you find them on your watch.

You can disconnect Garmin at any time in the app settings or in Garmin Connect. Activities received from Garmin are then deleted within 30 days.

Use of artificial intelligence

UltraMe uses Claude, Anthropic’s artificial intelligence, to:

  • create your plan and adjust it every week;
  • have Kova, your coach, answer your messages and change your plan when you ask.

To do this, I send Claude your running, training and health data, your messages to Kova and, if you connected Garmin, your Garmin activities. Never your name or email.

Under its commercial terms, Anthropic is not allowed to train its models on this data. I don’t train any AI model on your data either.

Before you connect Garmin, the app asks for your explicit consent for your Garmin activities to be processed by AI. You can withdraw this consent at any time by disconnecting Garmin.

The leaderboard

The leaderboard is on by default: other runners only see your username, your profile photo if you added one, and your Vims. You can turn it off anytime in settings to stop appearing on it.

Your country comes from your phone’s region settings (not your location). It’s only used to filter the regional leaderboard and is never shown.

How long I keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your information is erased within 30 days. Payment records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own legal obligations.

Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • access your information and get a copy in a common format;
  • have it corrected;
  • delete your account and all your data, right in the app;
  • withdraw your consent.

For any request, write to edouard.verreault@outlook.com. I’ll answer within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with my answer, you can contact Québec’s Commission d’accès à l’information.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored in protected services. Only I have access, with a password and two-factor authentication.

If a privacy incident presents a risk of serious harm, I’ll notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information.

Minimum age

UltraMe is for people 18 and over. I don’t knowingly collect information about minors.

Your plan isn’t medical advice

UltraMe plans are training plans. They don’t replace advice from a health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. For an important change, I’ll let you know in the app or by email.

Write to edouard.verreault@outlook.com
