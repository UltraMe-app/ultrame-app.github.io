Support

A question, a bug or a problem with your subscription? Email me at edouard.verreault@outlook.com and I’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

Cancel or manage my subscription

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → UltraMe. The subscription is managed by Apple; you can cancel at any time and it stays active until the end of the paid period.

Get my subscription back on a new phone

Sign in with the same UltraMe account and the same Apple ID, then tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen.

Delete my UltraMe account and data

In the UltraMe app: Settings → Delete my account. Your account and all your data (profile, plan, check-ins, messages to Kova, photo, Garmin activities) are erased within 30 days. Payment records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their legal obligations.

Don’t have the app anymore? Email edouard.verreault@outlook.com from your account’s address, with the subject “Delete my account”.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in your iPhone settings (Apple) or in the Google Play Store (Android).

My plan and my health

UltraMe plans are training plans, not medical advice. If you have pain or an injury, see a health professional.
